CHINESE SELF-STUDY TUTOR - QUICK START

Installation scope: user

1. Ask naturally, or invoke the Skill explicitly:
   $chinese-self-study-tutor <your request>

2. Add a response depth when useful:
   quickest — direct answer or correction with pinyin
   simple   — answer plus the key reason or example
   normal   — compact lesson with breakdown, notes, and practice
   detailed — deeper explanation and targeted practice
   full     — reusable study unit with broader practice

3. Try one of these prompts:
   $chinese-self-study-tutor normal: Correct this diary entry and explain the key mistakes.
   $chinese-self-study-tutor detailed: Explain 了 with pinyin and examples.
   $chinese-self-study-tutor full: Build a lesson, quiz, and answer key from 东西南北.
   $chinese-self-study-tutor Practise ordering coffee with me by voice.
   $chinese-self-study-tutor Explain 烛 through useful words before explaining the character.

Defaults:
- Explanations are in English; ask for German when needed.
- Taught Chinese includes tone-marked pinyin unless you ask to omit it.
- Work at normal and above is archived as one HTML file under ./archive/.
- Source files are not changed.
